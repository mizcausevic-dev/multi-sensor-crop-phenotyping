# Safeguards

Multi-Sensor Crop Phenotyping Platform. Conceptual, source-informed working brief. Not a certified engineering design.

## What the film is allowed to say

The mechanism source is USDA Agricultural Research Service project 448131, "Dynamic, Data-Driven, Sustainable, and Resilient Crop Production Systems for the U.S.", a cooperative agreement. The public project page was checked on 20 Oct 2026:

https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/project/?accnNo=448131

That page supports an example of a multi-sensor platform on an unmanned ground vehicle, intended to cover three plots, with route planning, image cataloguing, and pod detection for soybean, pea, and dry bean.

## What it must not say

- Phenotyping in this film estimates observable traits. It does not read genotype.
- It does not predict yield, and it does not claim that a pod count determines yield.
- The source project also lists yield models and genomic selection as separate objectives. Those are not results, and this film does not depict them.
- No accuracy percentage, confidence interval, or universal model.
- No camera, GNSS, vehicle, or boom specification. Housings stay closed. Interiors are omitted.
- Geometry, clocks, environmental words, the 100-cell grid, and every pod count are illustrative.
- No named person. The human reference is a role, not a portrait and not an official ground truth.

## Numbers that are tied together on purpose

Coverage grid: 57 vegetation, 23 soil, 20 unobserved, out of 100 cells.

Coverage is 57/80 on observed cells. The 20 unobserved cells stay out of the ratio.

Five-plant subset: 26 sensor-visible pods, 33 human-reference pods. The difference is 7, and each of those 7 is an unobserved pod.

Plants drawn up close are S2 (4 visible, 3 occluded), S3 (5 visible, 0 occluded), and S4 (3 visible, 3 occluded). S1 and S5 are marked off screen.

Visit V4 on the history chart uses this same coverage fraction and the same 26 and 33. Visit V5 (38 and 47) is a later illustration on the same five plants, not a second study.

## Picture rules

- Cyan is the route, the shared clock, and the signal path.
- Gold is stored energy, actuation, and heat.
- Violet is sensing, occlusion, and trait state.
- Slate is structure. Plant tissue stays green or tan.
- Plates are tagged HARDWARE, SCHEMATIC, or MATH OVERLAY.
- A schematic section is not a physical disassembly.
- Enlarged scale, slow motion, and time compression are labeled on the frames where they apply.
- Frames 960 through 1019 repeat one picture. The transport clock outside the canvas keeps moving.

## Qualitative balance

Stored vehicle energy goes to motion, sensors, and compute. Heat from the motors and the computer stays in the account and is not given a watt value. Sunlight on the canopy is a separate input to the crop. Images, environment, position, and trait estimates come out. Unobserved leaves and occluded pods stay in the field.
