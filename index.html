Safeguards and how to read this

  • Cyan: route, clock, and signal path
  • Gold: stored energy, actuation, heat
  • Violet: sensing, occlusion, trait state
  • Slate: structure and hardware housings

Colors are explanatory overlays. A violet ring on a housing means this is a sensor, not that the housing emits light. Gold on a wheel hub means actuation. Plant tissue stays a muted green or tan.

What is hardware, what is a diagram

Plates are tagged. HARDWARE is the vehicle, boom, calibration board, and crop. SCHEMATIC is a sectional explanation, not a physical disassembly. MATH OVERLAY is the coverage ratio, the comparison table, and the visit charts. Those numbers are illustrative. They are not measurements from a trial.

Labels you should see when they apply

  • Enlarged scale. The canopy is magnified relative to the field view.
  • Slow motion. The canopy scan is slowed so occlusion stays readable. It is not a field speed.
  • Time compression. Five example visits stand in for a longer growing period.
  • Hold. From frame 960 through 1019 the picture is repeated.

Claims this film does not make

  • No genotype or breeding-value readout.
  • No yield prediction.
  • No accuracy percentage and no universal model.
  • No camera, GNSS, or vehicle specification.

Worked example

The coverage grid is 100 cells: 57 vegetation, 23 soil, 20 unobserved. Coverage is 57/80 = 0.71 on observed cells. The five-plant table totals 26 sensor-visible pods and 33 human-reference pods. The difference is 7, and each of those 7 is an unobserved pod. The full table and the source boundary are in the film and in safeguards-Cursor.md.
